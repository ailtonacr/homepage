## Why centralize identity?

Separate accounts for every product create friction and extra maintenance. Ailton Dev Account provides a shared identity with a consistent experience across applications.

## A separation that matters

Authentication and authorization aren't the same as business data. The identity provider handles sessions, identity, and protocols; each application keeps its own rules.

OIDC, PKCE, sign-in redirects, and logout aren't just infrastructure details. They're part of the experience.

## A technical system should still feel human

Even an identity screen should feel clear, accessible, and familiar. The design adds personality while respecting the provider's native security flows.
