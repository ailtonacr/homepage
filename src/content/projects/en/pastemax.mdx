## The problem

Copying a link, a snippet of code, or a note should be simple. In practice, that information often gets stuck on the device where it was copied.

PasteMax explores an uninterrupted experience between devices without treating the cloud as a requirement. The first principle is simple: useful content should remain accessible on the device itself.

## How the architecture fits together

The app is a PWA with local persistence. When two devices share a network, LAN communication can use WebRTC. The Plus plan also offers remote synchronization through WebSocket, with content encrypted by the client before it is sent.

These paths complement one another. Free prioritizes the local experience; Plus adds cloud sync and retention according to its plan.

## What I'm still learning

Synchronization involves reconciliation, sessions, offline behavior, plan limits, and differences across mobile browsers. PasteMax is evolving with a consistent goal: keep the experience simple, even when the engineering isn't.
