## An initial question

How can someone keep track of their progress without turning the experience into a complicated spreadsheet?

MeuShape explores this question as a product in development, focused on helping people organize routines and relevant information over time.

## Thinking through the solution

The architecture favors a **Django-first** backend and considers **LangGraph** for workflows that genuinely benefit from agents. Establishing a stable foundation comes before ambitious automation.

The experience stays at the center. Small interactions should make sense without technical knowledge.

## What's next?

The product is evolving in stages, with a clear distinction between shipped decisions and ideas still being explored. This page does not promise medical diagnoses or health outcomes.
