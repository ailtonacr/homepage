## From planning to a product

Tá Valendo is a product under development and a space to put architectural decisions, workflows, and service integrations into practice.

## What matters in the process

Building a feature shouldn't erase the reasoning behind earlier decisions. Documentation, clear criteria, and traceability make an implementation easier to revisit.

The work is ongoing. This page describes the proposal and engineering challenges without claiming unfinished features are already available.
